# Virelle Color Palette

## Purpose

This document defines the official color direction for Virelle branding, packaging, website design, photography, and marketing materials.

The goal is to create a visual identity that feels:

- Luxury
- Modern
- Elegant
- Timeless
- Radiant

---

# Primary Color Palette

---

# Pearl Ivory

## Color Meaning

Represents:

- Clean beauty
- Soft luxury
- Elegance
- Purity

## Usage

Primary background color.

Used for:

- Packaging
- Website backgrounds
- Brand materials

---

# Champagne Gold

## Color Meaning

Represents:

- Luxury
- Warmth
- Premium quality

## Usage

Accent color.

Used for:

- Logo details
- Bottle accents
- Packaging details

---

# Warm Sand Beige

## Color Meaning

Represents:

- Natural beauty
- Comfort
- Skin tones
- Wellness

## Usage

Used for:

- Secondary backgrounds
- Marketing visuals
- Photography styling

---

# Deep Espresso

## Color Meaning

Represents:

- Sophistication
- Confidence
- Luxury

## Usage

Used for:

- Text
- Contrast
- Premium details

---

# Pearl Glow

## Color Meaning

Represents:

- Radiance

- Light reflection

---

# Colors To Avoid

Avoid:

❌ Neon colors

❌ Extremely bright colors

❌ Cheap-looking metallics

❌ Overly playful palettes

Reason:

These conflict with Virelle's timeless luxury identity.

---

# Packaging Direction

The ideal Virelle packaging should feel:

- Heavy
- Smooth
- Minimal
- Elegant

Possible materials:

- Frosted glass
- Matte finishes
- Soft-touch labels
- Gold accents

---

# Brand Visual Rule

Every Virelle design decision should pass this test:

"Would this look beautiful displayed on a luxury bathroom counter?"

If the answer is no, reconsider the design.
