# Ingredients to Avoid for the Hero Body Oil

These ingredients may have uses in other products, but they do not fit the goals for Virelle's signature fast-absorbing luxury body oil.

- Mineral Oil (brand positioning)
- Olive Oil (heavier skin feel)
- Castor Oil (too thick)
- Coconut Oil (can feel greasy in leave-on body oils)

---

# Virelle Ingredient Philosophy

Every ingredient selected for a Virelle formula must satisfy four principles:

1. Deliver visible performance.
2. Support healthy, nourished skin.
3. Feel luxurious during application.
4. Contribute to an elevated sensory experience.

If an ingredient does not enhance performance, skin health, or the overall user experience, it should not be included simply because it is trendy.
