# Customer Reviews & Market Insights

## Purpose

This document collects real customer feedback from competing luxury body care brands. The goal is to identify recurring compliments, frustrations, and unmet needs so Virelle can create products that solve real problems.

---

# What Customers Love

## Fast Absorption

Customers consistently praise products that absorb quickly and allow them to get dressed shortly after application.

Importance:
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Opportunity for Virelle:
Develop formulas that nourish deeply while leaving minimal residue.

---

## Silky Texture

Customers enjoy body oils that feel smooth and elegant rather than greasy.

Importance:
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Opportunity:
Create a signature "silk touch" finish that becomes recognizable as a Virelle product.

---

## Healthy Glow

Consumers love radiant skin that looks moisturized without appearing oily.

Importance:
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Opportunity:
Develop Virelle's signature "Glass Skin Body Glow."

---

## Luxury Fragrance

Customers enjoy body products that smell like fine perfume and layer well with other fragrances.

Importance:
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Opportunity:
Develop sophisticated scent collections inspired by luxury perfumes rather than overly sweet or artificial fragrances.

---

## Beautiful Packaging

Premium packaging increases perceived value and makes products feel gift-worthy.

Importance:
⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

Opportunity:
Design elegant, timeless bottles that customers are proud to display.

# Common Complaints

## Too Greasy

Many body oils leave skin feeling oily or sticky.

Virelle Solution:
Prioritize fast-absorbing ingredients and balanced textures.

---

## Transfers Onto Clothing

Customers dislike products that stain clothing or leave residue on sheets.

Virelle Solution:
Develop formulas that absorb quickly and minimize surface residue while maintaining a healthy glow.

---

## Fragrance Doesn't Last

Many products lose their scent quickly.

Virelle Solution:
Create fragrance systems that provide a luxurious experience while remaining skin-friendly.

---

## Weak Glow

Some body oils moisturize well but don't deliver the radiant finish customers expect.

Virelle Solution:
Develop a signature luminous finish that enhances healthy-looking skin.

---

## Feels Heavy

Rich oils can feel uncomfortable in warm weather or during the day.

Virelle Solution:
Balance nourishing oils with lightweight emollients for year-round comfort.

---

# Customer Wishlist

Customers frequently express interest in products that:

- Absorb within minutes.
- Leave skin soft all day.
- Create a luxurious glow.
- Smell expensive.
- Layer well with perfume.
- Work on all skin tones.
- Don't leave greasy fingerprints.
- Come in elegant packaging.
- Feel like a spa treatment at home.
