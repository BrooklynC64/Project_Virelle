# Price Research

## Purpose

This document analyzes the pricing strategies of premium and luxury body care brands to determine Virelle's ideal price positioning.

---

# Pricing Categories

## Drugstore

Examples:
- Dove
- Vaseline
- Nivea

Average Price:
$6–15

Target Customer:
Budget-conscious shoppers.

---

## Prestige

Examples:
- Sol de Janeiro
- Kopari
- OUAI

Average Price:
$28–42

Target Customer:
Beauty enthusiasts looking for premium experiences.

---

## Luxury

Examples:
- OSEA
- Nécessaire
- L'Occitane

Average Price:
$40–65

Target Customer:
Customers willing to invest in quality ingredients and elevated experiences.

---

## Ultra Luxury

Examples:
- Augustinus Bader
- La Mer
- Dior Beauty

Average Price:
$70+

Target Customer:
Luxury skincare collectors and high-income consumers.

# Virelle Position

Target Category:

Luxury Prestige

Target Retail Price:

Body Oil (100 mL): $42–48

Future Products

Body Butter: $38–45

Body Scrub: $30–38

Body Wash: $28–35

Body Mist: $36–42

Gift Sets: $95–150

---

# Why Customers Pay More

Customers are willing to spend more when products offer:

- Visible results
- Beautiful packaging
- Premium fragrance
- Pleasant texture
- High-quality ingredients
- Strong brand identity
- Consistent performance

---

# Pricing Strategy

Virelle will compete on value rather than price.

Instead of being the cheapest option, Virelle will justify its pricing through:

- High-performance formulations.
- Elegant packaging.
- Sophisticated fragrance development.
- Premium ingredients.
- A memorable luxury experience.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Position Virelle as a brand that customers confidently repurchase because they believe the quality and experience justify the investment.
