# Virelle Wear Testing

## Product

# Purpose

This document evaluates how Virelle body oil performs during real-world use.

The goal is to measure:

- Absorption speed
- Skin feel
- Glow appearance
- Residue level
- Transfer potential
- Hydration
- Long-term comfort

A luxury body oil must not only look beautiful immediately after application—it must feel comfortable throughout the day.

---

# Testing Guidelines

Each test should evaluate:

✓ How the product feels

✓ How the skin looks

✓ How long the finish lasts

✓ Whether the product interferes with clothing

✓ Whether the customer would enjoy daily use

# Application Test

## Test Method

Apply a measured amount of product to clean skin.

Recommended test areas:

- Arm
- Leg
- Shoulder
- Hand

Record:

- Amount applied
- Spreadability
- Absorption time
- Final finish

# Residue Testing

## Goal

Measure remaining surface oil after absorption.

Evaluate:

- Does skin feel sticky?
- Does skin feel smooth?
- Is there excess oil?
- Can residue be removed with a towel?

---

## Target Result

Low residue while maintaining glow.

---

# Transfer Testing

## Goal

Evaluate whether product transfers onto clothing or fabrics.

## Test Method

After application:

1. Allow product to absorb.
2. Press clean fabric against skin.
3. Observe transfer.

Test fabrics:

- Cotton
- Silk-like fabric
- Dark clothing
