# Virelle Signature Body Oil
## Formula Version 1.0

**Status:** Prototype
**Date Created:** July 2026
**Formulator:** Virelle R&D

---

# Objective

Develop a luxury body oil that delivers:

- Fast absorption
- Silky application
- Glass skin glow
- Healthy, nourished skin
- Minimal greasy residue
- Elegant fragrance
- Luxury feel

---

# Formula (100%)

## Performance Base (70%)

### Hemisqualane — 35%

Purpose:
- Creates the signature silky feel
- Absorbs quickly
- Reduces greasy after-feel
- Helps minimize transfer

---

### Squalane — 20%

Purpose:
- Supports the skin barrier
- Lightweight hydration
- Softens skin
- Adds healthy radiance

---

### Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride — 15%

Purpose:
- Improves glide
- Creates a luxurious texture
- Balances the lightweight feel

---

# Skin Nutrition (25%)

### Jojoba Oil — 15%

Purpose:
- Mimics the skin's natural oils
- Deep nourishment
- Improves softness

---

### Meadowfoam Seed Oil — 10%

Purpose:
- Long-lasting moisture
- Smooth finish
- Excellent stability

---

# Signature Experience (5%)

### Vitamin E — 0.5%

Purpose:
- Antioxidant
- Helps protect the oils from oxidation

---

### Fragrance — 1%

Purpose:
- Signature Virelle scent

---

### Pearl Mica — 3.5%

Purpose:
- Creates the signature Virelle glow
- Provides a refined luminous finish
- Cosmetic-grade only

---

# Why This Formula

The formula is built around four goals:

1. Luxury skin feel
2. Healthy skin
3. Fast absorption
4. Elegant glow

Each ingredient contributes to at least one of these goals.

---

# Manufacturing Process

1. Sanitize all equipment and containers.
2. Weigh each ingredient accurately.
3. Combine the liquid oils in a sanitized beaker.
4. Mix slowly until uniform.
5. Premix the mica with a small amount of the oil blend to remove clumps.
6. Add the mica mixture back into the main batch.
7. Add Vitamin E.
8. Add fragrance last.
9. Stir gently for several minutes.
10. Fill bottles and allow the formula to rest for 24 hours before evaluation.
