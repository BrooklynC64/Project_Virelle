# Formula Direction

## Concept

"Luxury dry oil with glass-skin radiance."

The ideal experience:

- Applies like silk
- Absorbs quickly

- Does not feel greasy

# Formula Structure

## Performance Base

### Hemisqualane

Role:

Primary fast-absorbing emollient.

Benefits:

- Dry-touch finish
- Silky texture
- Reduces oily after-feel

Target Range:

30–40%

---

### Squalane

Role:

Skin conditioning and softness.

Benefits:

- Supports skin barrier
- Adds smoothness
- Improves glow

Target Range:

15–25%

---

### Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride

Role:

Texture enhancer.

Benefits:

- Improves spreadability
- Creates cushion
- Balances formula

Target Range:

10–20%

---

### Coco-Caprylate/Caprate

Role:

Luxury lightweight emollient.

Benefits:

- Silicone-like slip
- Elegant finish
- Faster absorption

Target Range:

5–15%
