# Virelle Signature Body Oil

## Project Name

Virelle Glass Skin Body Oil

---

# Project Goal

Develop a luxury body oil that delivers an elegant glow, absorbs quickly, nourishes the skin, and creates a premium sensory experience.

The product should become the flagship of the Virelle brand.

---

# Product Vision

Customers should immediately notice:

• Silky application

• Fast absorption

• Healthy-looking skin

• Soft, smooth finish

• Luxurious fragrance

• Beautiful glow

The product should feel expensive from the first pump to the last drop.

---

# Customer Experience

The customer should think:

"This feels like luxury."

"My skin looks incredible."

"I've never used a body oil that feels like this."

# Formula Goals

The formula should:

✓ Absorb within 2–5 minutes

✓ Leave minimal surface residue

✓ Feel silky instead of greasy

✓ Improve skin softness

✓ Support the skin barrier

✓ Layer well with perfume

✓ Work year-round

✓ Be suitable for most skin types
