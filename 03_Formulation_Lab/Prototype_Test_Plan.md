# Virelle Prototype Test Plan

## Product

Virelle Signature Body Oil

---

# Purpose

This document outlines the testing strategy for developing the final Virelle body oil formula.

The goal is to compare different formula directions and identify the best balance of:

- Luxury feel
- Fast absorption
- Glow
- Hydration
- Low residue
- Customer experience

---

# Testing Philosophy

A great formula is not created by adding more ingredients.

A great formula is created by understanding:

- What each ingredient contributes
- How ingredients interact
- What customers value most

Each prototype will test a specific goal.

---

# Prototype A: Glass Glow Formula

## Goal

Create the most visually radiant formula.

## Focus

Maximum glow and luxurious appearance.

## Characteristics

Expected:

- High radiance
- Rich skin appearance
- Strong luxury feel

## Potential Adjustments

Increase:

- Glow-enhancing ingredients
- Nourishing oils

Monitor:

- Absorption speed
- Residue

---

# Prototype B: Dry Satin Formula

## Goal

Create the fastest absorbing formula.

## Focus

Lightweight, elegant, everyday wear.

## Characteristics

Expected:

- Quick absorption
- Minimal transfer
- Smooth finish

## Potential Adjustments

Increase:

- Lightweight emollients

Reduce:

- Heavy oils

Monitor:

- Whether glow remains visible

---

# Prototype C: Signature Virelle Formula

## Goal

Create the perfect balance.

## Focus

Luxury + performance.

## Characteristics

Expected:

- Silky application
- Visible glow
- Fast absorption
- Healthy skin finish

This formula should represent the Virelle identity.

# Testing Process

## Step 1

Create small test batches.

Recommended:

30–100 grams per prototype.

---

## Step 2

Record:

- Formula percentages
- Ingredient changes
- Texture observations

---

## Step 3

Perform wear testing.

Evaluate:

- Application
- Absorption
- Glow
- Transfer
- Skin feel

---

## Step 4

Select the strongest formula direction.

The winner becomes the foundation for the next revision.

---

# Decision Criteria

The final Virelle body oil should achieve:

★★★★★ Luxury feel

★★★★★ Beautiful glow

★★★★★ Fast absorption

★★★★★ Comfortable daily wear

★★★★★ Memorable fragrance

---

# Development Notes

The goal is not to create the heaviest or richest oil.

The goal is to create the most refined experience.

Virelle should feel like:

"Luxury skincare for the entire body."
