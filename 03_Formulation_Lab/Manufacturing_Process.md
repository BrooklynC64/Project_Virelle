# Virelle Manufacturing Process

## Product

Virelle Signature Body Oil

## Purpose

This document outlines the standard manufacturing process for producing Virelle body oil prototypes.

The goal is to create a consistent, safe, and repeatable process that maintains:

- Formula quality
- Ingredient integrity
- Luxury texture
- Product performance

---

Every Virelle product should be made with:

✓ Precision

✓ Clean processes

✓ Quality ingredients

✓ Consistent measurements

✓ Detailed documentation

---

# Equipment Preparation

## Required Equipment

- Precision digital scale
- Glass mixing beaker
- Glass stirring rod
- Pipettes
- Gloves
- Sanitizing solution
- Filling containers
- Final product bottles

---

# Step 1: Sanitation

Before production:

1. Clean all equipment.
2. Sanitize all surfaces.
3. Allow equipment to fully dry.
4. Wear gloves during production.
5. Ensure containers are clean before filling.

Purpose:

Reduce contamination risk and maintain product quality.

---

# Step 2: Ingredient Preparation

Before mixing:

1. Confirm all ingredients are correct.
2. Check ingredient appearance.
3. Record batch numbers.
4. Measure each ingredient accurately.

Each batch should have:

- Date created
- Formula version
- Ingredient lot numbers
- Final batch size

---

# Step 3: Oil Phase Mixing

Combine the main oil ingredients:

Order:

1. Hemisqualane
2. Squalane
3. Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride
4. Botanical oils

Mix slowly until completely uniform.

Goal:

Create a smooth, consistent oil base.

---

# Step 4: Add Supporting Ingredients

Add:

- Vitamin E
- Fragrance

Mix gently.

Avoid excessive agitation to prevent unnecessary air introduction.

---

# Step 5: Add Glow Components

For glow versions:

Add cosmetic-grade mica.

Process:

1. Pre-mix mica with a small amount of oil.
2. Break up any clumps.
3. Slowly incorporate into the main batch.
4. Mix until evenly distributed.

---

# Step 6: Quality Check

Before filling, evaluate:

## Appearance

✓ Clear or evenly dispersed

✓ No separation

✓ No particles

---

## Texture

Evaluate:

- Spreadability
- Silkiness
- Absorption speed
- After-feel

---

## Fragrance

Evaluate:

- Strength
- Balance
- Longevity

---

# Step 7: Filling

Process:

1. Fill sanitized bottles.
2. Install pumps or caps.
3. Label each batch.
4. Record final weight.
