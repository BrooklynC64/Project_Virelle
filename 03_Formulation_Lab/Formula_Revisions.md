# Virelle Formula Revision History

## Purpose

This document tracks every change made during Virelle product development.

Each formula revision should document:

- What changed
- Why it changed
- What improved
- What problems appeared
- What we learned

The goal is to continuously improve product performance while maintaining the Virelle luxury experience.

---

# Formula Development Rules

## Rule 1: Change Intentionally

Each revision should have a specific purpose.

Examples:

- Improve absorption
- Increase glow
- Reduce residue
- Improve fragrance performance
- Increase skin nourishment

---

## Rule 2: Change One Major Variable at a Time

Changing too many ingredients at once makes it difficult to understand what caused improvement or problems.

---

## Rule 3: Document Everything

No test result is wasted.

A formula that fails teaches us what direction not to take.
